The Story

A 2.50% loan you can ask to take over

This home comes with something most listings don't: a VA loan at 2.50%, with about $360,228 left on it. A qualifying buyer can ask about assuming that loan instead of taking out a new one at today's rates. You don't have to be a veteran to qualify.

The home itself is a 2011-built, two-story in the Brookmont community. Granite island kitchen with stainless steel appliances, hardwood floors, a gas fireplace, new carpet, and fresh paint throughout.

Upstairs, the primary suite has a 5-piece ensuite bath and a walk-in closet that opens straight into the laundry room. There's also a loft, which can flex into a home office, a playroom, a media room, whatever fits your life.

Life Here

Close to the lake, close to work

Sunset Beach Park on Lake Stevens is about a 3-minute drive, or a 25-minute walk. Swimming, boating, waterskiing, and mountain views of Mt. Baker and Mt. Pilchuck on a clear day.

The yard is fenced and low-maintenance, so weekends stay about the lake instead of the lawn.

Lake Stevens has an easy enough commute to Boeing, Everett Naval Station, and the rest of Snohomish County's job centers for most people working in the area.
